# Problem Statement — SmartStock AI

Quick-commerce companies lose revenue every day — not because 
of bad products, but because no one saw the stockout coming.

## The Problem

Without real-time inventory visibility and accurate demand 
forecasting, businesses face:

- 📦 Products going out of stock without warning
- ❌ Orders getting cancelled = direct revenue loss
- 🏭 Warehouses either overstocked or understocked
- 📈 Weekend demand spikes consistently missed
- 🔍 No way to identify which warehouse is failing

## The Result

Operational chaos, unhappy customers, and measurable revenue 
leakage — all of which could have been prevented with the 
right data.

## The Solution

**SmartStock AI** solves this.

By building an end-to-end supply chain analytics system across 
**10 warehouses**, **1,00,000+ orders**, and **6 product 
categories** — this project identifies where the losses are 
happening, why they are happening, and what needs to happen next.
